## 🌐 Overview
**GO2ABROAD Registry** is an open-access statutory intelligence engine decoding cross-border corporate formation, multi-tier tax systems, and corporate banking compliance across **80+ jurisdictions**.

Built upon **23,795+ structured compliance dossiers**, the registry standardizes the entire enterprise lifecycle across 6 stages:
1. **Pre-Incorporation & UBO Transparency**
2. **Statutory Formation & First Board Resolutions**
3. **Tax Identity & Indirect Tax Nexus**
4. **Corporate Banking Rails & EMI Whitelists**
5. **Annual Governance & Compulsory Audit Thresholds**
6. **Corporate Income Tax (CIT) & Cross-Border Reporting**

---

## 📂 Open Data Architecture & Schemas

The registry provides open-source indices, multi-language dictionaries, and statutory matrices:

* **Catalog Index (`catalog_index.min.json`)**: Lightweight indices optimized for client-side search and edge filtering.
* **11-Language Dictionaries (`dimension_i18n.json`)**: Localized entity structures, industry categories, and sovereign jurisdiction mapping.
* **Master Dossiers (`dossiers/*.jsonl`)**: Comprehensive 6-stage compliance datasets for global jurisdictions.
